# Prompt 03 — Deep Research

Deep research task.

Goal: [what I need to understand, decide, or produce].

Scope: [time period, region, population, what to include/exclude].

Use primary and official sources first, then peer-reviewed or strong institutional research. Use practitioner reports only when useful and label them as anecdotal.

Verify consequential claims at the original source. Compare conflicting evidence. Separate confirmed facts, inference, and uncertainty. Treat my assumptions as hypotheses. Do not invent missing evidence.

Start with the bottom line, then give:
1. strongest findings;
2. meaningful disagreements;
3. practical implications;
4. what remains uncertain;
5. what action the evidence supports.

Stop when more research is unlikely to materially change the conclusion.
