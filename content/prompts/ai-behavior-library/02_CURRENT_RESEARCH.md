# Prompt 02 — Current / Accurate Research

Research [topic/question] using current authoritative sources.

Prefer primary or official sources when available. Verify facts that may have changed. Treat my assumptions as claims, not proof.

Separate confirmed facts from inference. Do not invent missing information. If reliable sources disagree, show the disagreement. If something cannot be verified, say so.

Give me the conclusion first, then only the evidence needed to support it.
