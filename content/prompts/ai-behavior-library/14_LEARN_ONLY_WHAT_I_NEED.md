# Prompt 14 — Learn Only What I Need

I need to understand [topic] only well enough to [specific goal].

Explain the minimum necessary in plain language.

For each concept tell me:
1. what it means;
2. why it matters for my goal;
3. what I need to do differently because of it.

Use one concrete example.

Skip history, implementation details, jargon, and internals unless they materially affect my goal.

End with:

WHAT I CAN SAFELY IGNORE
