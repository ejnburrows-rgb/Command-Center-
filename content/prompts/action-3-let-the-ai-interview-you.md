INSTEAD of trying to explain everything upfront, flip it — tell the AI to interview you.

THE EXACT PROMPT:
"Interview me. Work with me to identify the core problem we are solving, and who it is and isn't for. Work through any key decisions together. Once finished, summarize it back to me as an implementation spec."

WHY THIS MATTERS:
This pulls out details you did not know you needed to think about. Answer every question thoughtfully — do not rush through it.

VARIATION (voice-first planning):
"Interview me about this feature. Here is my brain-dump: [PASTE VOICE TRANSCRIPT]. Ask me ONE question at a time to fill in the gaps. When finished, summarize it as an implementation spec I can approve or edit."

VARIATION (for new projects):
"You are a senior product architect. I need you to interview me about the CRM feature I want to build. Ask me ONE question at a time. Do not move to the next question until I answer. When finished, produce a spec.md file with these sections: Goal, Who uses it, Inputs, Outputs, Rules, Edge Cases, Out of Scope, Minimum Tests. Start with: 'What feature are we building today?'"
