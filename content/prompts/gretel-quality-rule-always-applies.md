Whenever Gretel appears, she must look grounded and integrated:
- contact shadow
- ambient shadow
- warm color matching
- no white outline
- no hard glow
- no sticker look
- no cheap paper-poster effect

CANON APPEARANCE (never deviate):
golden wavy blonde hair with a RED BOW on top; blue eyes; pink blush cheeks; NO freckles;
blue pinafore with orange-floral embroidered hem; orange-and-pale-orange striped long-sleeve shirt;
white knee-high socks with a red-yellow band + scalloped red-dot lace; black mary-jane shoes; age ~5–7.

ART RULE: color + place, never redraw. Use Estela de Armas Plasencia's original line art, colorized in HD.
